Flite.com
A site that evolved with Flite’s growth

Design, branding, IA, icon design, development

Flite  2015

Showcasing Flite’s capabilities

As Flite expanded, its marketing site needed to keep up. The original site no longer reflected the sophistication of its technology or the value it provided to brands and publishers. I led the full redesign of Flite.com, creating a site that clearly conveyed the platform’s ability to build, optimize, and measure high-impact digital ads in real time.




The new site focused on Flite’s core strengths—Design Studio, Campaign Studio, and Report Studio—demonstrating how brands could seamlessly create, manage, and analyze ad performance across devices.

Driving the vision, design, and strategy

I oversaw every aspect of the redesign, from site architecture and content strategy to interaction design and visual identity. The goal was to create more than just a marketing site—it needed to be a compelling and dynamic tool that clearly communicated Flite’s value to prospective customers.




I streamlined the messaging to make complex technology more accessible, introduced high-quality visuals and interactive elements to showcase Flite’s real-time creative capabilities, and refined the site’s navigation for an intuitive, engaging experience. The redesign also featured full-frame imagery and a bold visual system, reinforcing Flite’s position as a leader in creative ad management.

Impact & results

The redesigned Flite.com became a critical asset for marketing and sales, increasing engagement with brands and publishers. By aligning the website with the evolution of the product, it strengthened Flite’s market positioning and played a key role in its eventual acquisition by Snapchat.

Key takeaways

This project reinforced the importance of keeping marketing aligned with product growth—as Flite’s offerings advanced, its web presence had to reflect that evolution. Clarity in content and design matters—concise messaging and strong visuals made the site more effective. And a website should be a strategic tool, not just a showcase—Flite.com became an essential driver of business growth and industry credibility.
