# Portfolio V2 quality rubric

The independent evaluator scores each dimension from 0–10. The final percentage is `total points / 70 × 100`. A release candidate passes only at **63/70 (90%) or higher**, with no individual dimension below 8.

## 1. Typographic character and hierarchy

Measures whether the site reproduces the reference’s quiet but decisive typography: oversized, compact display headlines; neutral sans-serif body copy; crisp mono metadata; restrained weights; and legible line lengths. A 10 requires immediately recognizable hierarchy at desktop and mobile without ornamental type effects.

## 2. Editorial grid and alignment

Measures the reference’s centered 1024px content field, consistent left edges, deliberate 680px reading column, two-column project index, and mathematically clean alignment between headlines, copy, captions, and media. A 10 has no accidental offsets or inconsistent gutters.

## 3. Whitespace, pacing, and scroll rhythm

Measures the reference’s generous vertical breathing room and repeated text→media→text cadence. A 10 preserves calm pacing across long cases, varies density intentionally, and avoids both cramped passages and arbitrary dead zones.

## 4. Media staging and composition

Measures full-bleed-in-column imagery, large cinematic crops, subtle corner radii, purposeful grids, and visual sequencing. A 10 makes every artifact feel deliberately framed, uses captions where needed, and maintains strong aspect ratios without distortion.

## 5. Chromatic restraint and surface treatment

Measures the near-white canvas, charcoal type, muted secondary text, sparse project-specific color fields, minimal borders, and low-decoration material language. A 10 feels warm, tactile, and restrained rather than generic or overly UI-like.

## 6. Navigation, motion, and interaction finish

Measures the reference’s simple persistent navigation, understated hover feedback, smooth transitions, and absence of distracting controls. A 10 provides clear orientation, polished focus/hover states, reduced-motion support, and fluid transitions that never compete with the work.

## 7. Content-led adaptation, fidelity, and responsive continuity

Measures whether the reference language has been extended around the supplied cases without forcing or editing them: exact wording and emphasis, correct study ordering and captions, useful metadata, and layouts that retain their visual intent from wide desktop through narrow mobile. A 10 has no substantive omissions or rewrites and no breakpoint where the editorial composition collapses.

## Evaluation evidence

The evaluator should inspect the home page and all four cases at approximately 1440px and 390px widths, check keyboard navigation and reduced-motion behavior, compare the result to the reference captures/data and Figma extracts, and list concrete deductions with page/section locations.
