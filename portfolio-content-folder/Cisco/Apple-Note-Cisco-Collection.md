# Cisco Collection

This case is simple.

There are two projects discussing customer insights.

The first project was the first dashboard, which provided an overview of how account managers prepare for meetings.

It also included process diagrams that explained the ideation and inception process.

After the first custom inside dashboard was successful, various teams saw it as a place where account managers could access tools and packages.

They wanted to insert their own tools or packages onto the platform to leverage their offerings.

This was like an advertisement, but instead of a typical ad, it was an offering.

In phase 2, I created a framework for sales operations teams to promote their offers to account managers.

There’s a third case study about the general visual design and aesthetics of the Cisco Reddy application.

However, it doesn’t have a linear story or many metrics.

I don’t know if it should be included, but I think the first step should be consolidating the two case studies into one strong case study.
