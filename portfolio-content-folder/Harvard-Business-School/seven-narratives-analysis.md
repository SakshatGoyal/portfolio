# Seven Narratives Hiding in the HBS Work
### An independent reading of `/HBS-research-project` **and the portfolio visual layer**, judged against the Caliber Standard

**Revision 2.** The first pass read only the research archive (chats, pipeline prompts, retrospectives, final-deliverable markdown). This revision adds a full read of `Harvard-Business-School/AI-Research-Architecture/` — every unique image in `assets/local-source`, `past-images-hbs`, and `published-derivatives`, the Notion case-study draft, the consistency-and-interpretive-depth PDF, and the model-evaluation mini case study. Three findings force real changes:

1. **A whole evidence layer was invisible in round 1: the model-evaluation program.** The consistency × interpretive-depth 2×2 (gpt-o1-pro / o3-high / 4.5 / 4o mapped against a "Desired Performance" zone), the five-level **Degrees of Interpretive Depth** scale (Literal → Contextual → Well-Reasoned → Intuitive → Imaginative), an **eight-iteration blind-test table** with per-model consistency percentages, and a mini case study documenting a 50%→65% repeat-run alignment gain and a tiered model-routing decision. This is not "prompting texture" — it is an *original measurement instrument*, and it upgrades one narrative from supporting material to a load-bearing limb.
2. **The craft verdict reverses.** Round 1 scored the whole body weak on marker 3.3 ("nothing to look at in 30 seconds"). The portfolio layer proves otherwise: a coherent visual system (mono annotation type, navy/crimson/warm-neutral palette, waffle-grid corpus visualizations, annotated real artifacts) that survives both the scan and the zoom. The scan-gate problem is now residual, not structural.
3. **The published narrative already exists — and it omits its own best story.** The Notion draft ("building an AI-enabled research architecture to structure extreme ambiguity") runs Problem → Decision → Outcome and lands two genuinely good lessons, but it presents the situational axes as a clean invention and never mentions the clustering failure that produced them. The strongest 3.2/3.4 material in the archive is currently left out of the telling.

**What the evidence now shows, in one sentence:** a solo designer took an unanswerable question — *what should an AI strategy assistant for senior executives be?* — calibrated the AI instruments before trusting them (blind-tested, measured, routed), built an industrial-scale provenance-disciplined research system (237 papers → 992 coded insight modules → situational axes → 4 archetypes × 4 themes → 16 tension scenarios → 7 tradeoff principles → MVP scoping options) for a real engagement (Intelligaia × Harvard D³), survived one major methodological failure, and then *crafted the system itself into a designed, scannable visual argument*.

## The seven narratives (revised)

### N1 — The Research Factory
*"I designed and operated an industrial system for qualitative research at a scale one person cannot read."*

**The story (unchanged in substance).** ~237 papers → 992 structured insight modules via a three-stage extraction chain, then multi-rubric coding: strategy classification (six decision settings), 21 binary research-question alignment tests, situational-axes scoring, interpretive scenario tagging — a `modules × dimensions` dataset with `[Empirical]/[Inferred]/[Speculative]` provenance throughout.

**What the visual layer changes.** The four pipeline-process graphics *already tell this story the right way*: each stage opens with a decision-first sentence in the first person with the reason attached ("To avoid studying 'executives' as an undifferentiated category, I built the source corpus from a structured inquiry grid…"; "To prevent the synthesis from blending executive behavior with AI opportunity, I decomposed the 3 research questions into 21 binary alignment tests…"), pairs a corpus-scale waffle visualization with one *real, zoomable artifact* (Insight Module 24 – C4-I4 with its inline provenance tags; the actual 21-question checklist with pass/fail marks). This is the "headers carry the argument" fix from round 1, already executed. The stage-1 drafts also fix the count drift — 237 and 992 are used consistently across the published layer.

**Caliber read (revised).**
- **3.1 strong; 3.4 strong** (unchanged — the annotated real module is unfakeable texture).
- **3.2 substantially improved in the telling**: the published framing narrates contested choices, not process steps. Still missing: the *why-not* layer (why multiple rubrics instead of one taxonomy; the 6-axes→4-axes prune; weights set by fiat) that would make each decision arguable.
- **3.3 now a genuine strength**: the waffle-grid system passes the scan test and rewards zoom (real module text, real checklist).
- **3.5 unchanged weakness**: the factory alone still has no business consequence.

**Verdict.** Upgraded from "essential substrate, fails standalone" to **the strongest visual act of the spine case** — but still an act, not the case.

### N2 — Ambiguity Into Direction
*"I was handed a question with no shape — and returned a decision a product team could act on."*

**The story (unchanged).** Zareen fiction → formal research questions → contrast-maximized sourcing grid designed against self-diagnosed bias → coded corpus → archetypes → 4×4 matrix → four explicit MVP scoping approaches with recommendations.

**What the visual layer changes.** The Notion draft *is* this narrative — same spine, same Problem/Decision/Outcome rhythm — which confirms the round-1 recommendation was the right instinct and is already half-built. The theme-selection graphics (Approaches 1–4 with highlighted rows/columns/T/cell) give the ending a scannable artifact: you can *see* the recommendation being made. Two published-layer weaknesses: the matrix tables carry emoji in every cell, which undercuts the register the rest of the system earns (a hiring manager scanning for rigor reads those as decoration), and the current draft's outcome section is one thin sentence ("helped fine-tune the voice of an AI system") — the causal chain still stops early.

**Caliber read (revised).** 3.1 strongest of all seven (unchanged). 3.8 strengthened — the four-approach ending is a visible decision, not a summary. 3.5 unchanged: impact still ends at the delivered recommendation, and the published draft should say so plainly rather than gesture at product influence it can't evidence.

**Verdict.** Still the **spine**. The Notion draft is its skeleton; what it's missing is N5's drama and N4's calibration act.

### N3 — The Honest Corpus
*"I made AI-scale research trustworthy — every claim traceable, every speculation labeled."*

**What the visual layer changes.** The provenance ethic turns out to be *visible in the shipped pixels*: the typical-insight-module graphic renders `[Empirical]` and `[Inferred]` tags inline; the 16 matrix-cell cards carry `[speculated]` markers on exactly the sections that outran the evidence; the mini case study closes with an unprompted scope note — "this is a reconstruction from project chat records and process notes, not a controlled external benchmark." That last sentence is the single most credibility-buying line in the entire portfolio layer, and it should survive every future edit.

**Caliber read (unchanged).** Strongest 3.4 of the seven; not a standalone case; the register everything else is written in.

**One new caution this narrative must police.** The people-problem illustration cards (covers, hero) are editorial-style illustrations whose reference moodboard sits in `sample-graphics/` — stock and editorial images in the same visual language. If those cards are AI-generated or commissioned in that referenced style, the case study should attribute them the same way it attributes everything else. For a body of work whose entire thesis is provenance discipline, an unattributed illustration layer is the one place an interrogator could turn the thesis against the author. Disclosure is cheap here and on-brand; silence is the only losing move.

### N4 — Calibrating the Machine *(substantially upgraded — this is the limb the round-1 analysis undersold)*
*"Before I let AI interpret research at scale, I measured whether it could — and built the instruments to measure with."*

**The story (rebuilt).** Round 1 framed this as "operator texture, walkthrough-only." The portfolio folder shows it was a *designed evaluation program* with artifacts:

- **A named problem.** Reasoning models were dependable but literal; creative models read latent meaning but wouldn't apply logic consistently across repeated datasets. The failure modes are specified: run-to-run variation, score flattening (a 17-criterion rubric initially collapsing every module to the same score), scale drift.
- **An original instrument.** The **Degrees of Interpretive Depth** scale — Literal / Contextual / Well-Reasoned / Intuitive / Imaginative — is an invented qualitative metric for something nobody hands you a benchmark for: *how deeply a model reads*. Paired with a plain-language consistency definition ("does the model produce the same result twice, given the same instruction and data"), it forms a 2×2 with a marked Desired Performance zone and the four models placed on it.
- **A real protocol.** Eight blind-test iterations, same goal held fixed, prompts and reference documents as the manipulated variable. The table shows honest movement in both directions: gpt-4o discontinued at ~45%; gpt-o1-pro parked at ~95% consistency but stuck at Literal ("improving interpretive depth proved challenging" — a benchmark, not a worker); gpt-4.5 climbing ~50%→~84% *while holding Intuitive* at iteration 05, then **declining** at 06 and 08 — with the stated lesson that "gains were not automatic; prompts and reference documents needed to operate as one deliberately designed system."
- **A decision with consequences.** A tiered model workflow — o3-mini-high as the high-volume tagger (50%→65% derived alignment), full o3 for escalation, GPT-4.5 for creative stress-testing, o1-pro for bulk throughput — instead of one universal model. This routing decision is what made the 992-module factory trustworthy.
- **The operator texture from round 1 folds in underneath**: persona instruments, anti-contamination guardrails, the parts-bin move, and the sycophancy episode (asked the model for honesty during the clustering crisis, got flattery, corrected by walking away).

**Caliber read (revised).**
- **3.2 now excellent**: model selection is framed as a falsifiable, evidence-backed decision a peer could contest — precisely the shape 3.2 rewards.
- **3.3 now strong**: the 2×2, the depth scale, and the iteration table are crafted, scannable, and zoom-proof.
- **3.5 now partially answered *inside* the narrative**: +15pp repeatability and a 43pp reduction in score concentration are honest, mechanism-attached numbers — small, real, and explained, which the standard explicitly prefers over big unexplained ones.
- **3.7 landmines, named**: the percentages are approximations reconstructed from chat records (the docx says so); "blind tests" needs a crisp definition the author can state in one sentence; and the model placements on the 2×2 must match the table story under questioning. Also reconcile the docx's "880 modules in scale target" with the published 992.
- **Why it still isn't the spine**: it is an instrumentation story in service of a research question. Alone, it's a methods paper; inside N2, it's the moment the hero checks the equipment before the climb — and in a 2026 hiring context (per §1's counter-current: visible, disclosed working-with-AI process is the one artifact that *adds* trust), it's the most differentiating single section in the whole case.

**Verdict.** Promoted from walkthrough-only texture to **a full act of the spine case with its own visual centerpiece** — likely the section a design leader remembers.

### N5 — When the Algorithm Failed
*"The clustering collapsed a week before deadline. I chose an instrument I could read — and shipped."*

**The story (unchanged).** HDBSCAN/UMAP sweep collapsing into undifferentiated clusters; the 8am April 10 pivot to hand-entered axis-value filter combinations; archetypes derived from readable distributions; the February personas buried by evidence.

**What the visual layer changes — two things, one good, one that must be fixed.**
- *Good:* the pivot now has a **beautiful artifact**. The triangulation graphic — four quadrant scatters → axis-combination intersections → "Who we find / Where we see them emerge / Roles they take" — *is the manual method, drawn*. Round 1 said this narrative lacked a visual; it doesn't anymore. The situational-axes graphic (one module walked through six axes to its scored profile) is the instrument the pivot reached for.
- *Must fix:* **the published Notion draft erases the failure.** It says "I created a novel approach to mapping activities" — true, but it presents the axes as a clean invention, with no trace of the dead quantitative branch that forced them. That's the exact "frictionless narrative" pattern 3.4 penalizes, and it discards the case's best drama. One honest paragraph restores it: *the algorithmic route couldn't discriminate and I couldn't verify it, the deadline was real, so I chose a method whose every step I could read — and that method is the one that produced the archetypes.*
- *Consistency note:* the axes appear as six in the situational-axes graphic (adding Knowledge Transferability and Market Dispersion) and four in the archive's tagging prompt; the triangulation intersections use the four structural ones. Decide the canonical telling before an interviewer notices.

**Caliber read.** 3.2 strongest of the seven (unchanged); 3.3 upgraded; 3.7 defused only by the honest telling (verifiability-over-sophistication, deadline real — never "it failed analytically").

**Verdict.** Still the **dramatic center** — and currently the biggest gap between what the archive proves and what the published draft says.

### N6 — Principles With Teeth

The same-question-two-answers device makes the tradeoff legible in seconds — the strongest pure scan artifact in the portfolio. The Notion draft also correctly connects the principles to their evidence base (the RQ-3 module lane from the routing stage), which quietly closes the derivation chain.

**Caliber read.** 3.6 (the multi-contributor method, team-scope), 3.2, and now 3.3 all strong; 3.5 still forward-looking ("helped fine-tune the voice of an AI system" is the honest ceiling until something ships against these).

**Verdict.** Still the **closing act** and the clearest originality claim. The cards should be the case study's most-shared image.

Harmless in a working folder, fatal on a live page: a reviewer who spots a wrong-project caption reads it as template-portfolio evidence, the exact §3.4 distrust trigger.

**Verdict.** Unchanged: the **credential layer**, one framing line plus a lined-up voucher — never the story.

## Recommendation: one spine, four acts, one register, one frame *(revised)*

Round 1 proposed "one spine, three organs." The portfolio layer both validates the spine choice — the Notion draft already *is* N2 — and forces one structural change: **N4 is promoted from walkthrough-only to a full act.**

- **Spine — N2 (Ambiguity Into Direction).** Keep the Notion draft's Problem→Decision→Outcome skeleton and its two closing lessons (the "move backward to define what an insight is" lesson and the engineer's "you've built a pipeline" trap — both are exactly the provenance-texture register the standard rewards).
- **Act 1 — the question and the grid** (compressed N1): the decision-first pipeline visuals already exist; add the why-not layer (rubric multiplicity, the axes prune, weights by fiat).
- **Act 2 — calibrating the machine (N4, new).** The 2×2, the Degrees of Interpretive Depth scale, the eight-iteration table, the tiered-routing decision. This is the 2026 differentiator: disclosed, measured, visible working-with-AI — placed *before* the corpus work so the reader trusts everything downstream. Pre-cash the 3.7 checks: define the blind-test protocol in one sentence, keep the "reconstruction, not benchmark" scope note, reconcile 880/992.
- **Act 3 — the crisis (N5).** Restore the omitted failure paragraph to the published draft; let the triangulation graphic carry the recovery. This is currently the largest divergence between evidence and telling, and the cheapest large win available.
- **Act 4 — the payoff (N2's matrix + N6).** Theme-selection approaches as the visible decision; the principle/counter-principle cards as the originality claim and the shareable image.
- **Register throughout — N3.** The provenance tags are already in the pixels; extend the same honesty to illustration attribution and keep the scope note.
- **Frame — N7.** One line of institutional context, captions fixed, one voucher lined up.

**Why this reads staff.** Altitude from N2, judgment from N5, measurement originality from N4, method multiplication from N6, trust from N3 — now with a visual system that passes the 30-second gate on its own. The remaining honest gap is downstream consequence, and the standard's own rule applies: state what could and couldn't be measured, and go get the D³/Intelligaia voucher that says what happened next.

**What to avoid (updated):**
- Publishing the axes as a clean invention with the algorithmic failure erased (current draft does this — undo it).
- Leading with the factory or the engagement (unchanged).
- Letting approximate percentages harden into precise claims ("~84%" stays approximate, the scope note stays).
- Shipping with the wrong-project captions, the emoji register, or unattributed illustrations — three small tells that each invite the one question this body of work least deserves: *did this person actually do this?* They did; the assets just have to stop suggesting otherwise.
