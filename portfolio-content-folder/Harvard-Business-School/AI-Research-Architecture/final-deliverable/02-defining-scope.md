## Next steps while defining scope

Here's an overview of all the problems, mapped by archetypes and across themes. The next step for the product team will involve exploring different combinations from the table to define the scope for an MVP.

### Please Note:

While these may appear as refined, isolated scenarios... In the process of building towards a special use case, it is inevitable to also design peripheral features that allow for more general purpose use cases.

### Overview Matrix

**People (Archetypes) x Themes:**

|  | Legacy Navigators | Urgent Executioners | Coordinated Builders | Principled Orchestrators |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| | *Designs enduring systems within slow-moving, legacy-laden environments, prioritizing future relevance over speed.* | *Acts urgently within rigid boundaries, balancing high-speed delivery with tightly enforced external constraints.* | *Thrives in modular yet interdependent systems, where value depends on precisely timed, cross-functional coordination.* | *Navigates long-horizon complexity through layered decision-making, emphasizing principled clarity over rapid action.* |
| **Relational Fragility** | Scale vs. Customization | Short-Term Gains vs. Long-Term Capability | Operational Independence vs. Strategic Partnerships | Psychological Safety vs. Hierarchical Dynamics |
| **Underestimated Complexity** | Transformation vs. Legacy Systems | Speed vs. Constraints | Internal Identity vs. Aggressive Growth | Intuitive Decisions vs. Structured Analytics |
| **Emotional Barriers to Change** | Emotional Resonance vs. Digital Innovation | Strategic Narratives vs. Evolving Realities | Proven Models vs. Disruptive Pricing | Cognitive Flexibility vs. Cognitive Inertia |
| **Technology and Human Tension** | Predictive Analytics vs. Human Intuition | Deployment vs. Governance | Craftsmanship vs. Brand Perception | Ethical Integrity vs. AI-Driven Efficiency |

**Theme Descriptions:**

- **Relational Fragility:** The struggle to grow while building trust and collaboration, potentially weakening execution and capability.
- **Underestimated Complexity:** Leaders misjudging complexity and struggling to build a strategy for changing landscapes.
- **Emotional Barriers to Change:** Challenges while bringing change, recalibration, and experimentation.
- **Technology and Human Tension:** Evolution of technology outpacing governance, trust, and human judgement safeguards.

Here you can chose to address different archetypes, for a particular use case.

**Our recommendation: Technology and Human Tension.**

*In this approach, the "Technology and Human Tension" row is highlighted across all four archetypes, meaning you would address:*
- Legacy Navigators: Predictive Analytics vs. Human Intuition
- Urgent Executioners: Deployment vs. Governance

## Approach 3: a T shaped approach

**Our recommendation: Coordinated Builders + Technology and Human Tension.**

**Caution:** Each problem statement is a massive design scope. A T-shaped approach could potentially explode your scope.
